## Proved scope

Under incidence, matching, location and common-frame assumptions, every admitted prime event has coordinate one-half. The signed-crease readout recovers exactly the zeta zeros on its declared critical-line domain, preserving position and multiplicity. These are universally quantified conclusions within the declared domains.

For an arbitrary original nontrivial zero, the full address retains d = Re ρ − ½. Universal recovery from Y alone is equivalent to d = 0 for every such zero. The package proves that equivalence and does not assert universal RH placement.
