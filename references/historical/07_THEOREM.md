# ENIAD prime-trace / zeta-zero correspondence
18 October 2026. Local conditional bridge theorem. Classical analytic result with an explicit ENIAD realization interface; no novelty, native-completion, individual-pairing or RH claim.

## Hypotheses
H1. A decoded family of source-bound records has exactly one record for every (p,k), p prime and integer k>=1, and no other records. Completeness means this universal condition, not a tested finite prefix.
H2. Its mathematical semantics assigns location p^k and weight log p exactly. Symbolic values are permitted; floating approximations alone do not satisfy exactness.
H3. Every admitted presentation/transport has a decoder recovering the same records, identities, revisions and joint relations. Partial sums use finite sets of records; exhaustion includes every record.
H4. Classical complex analysis and the meromorphic Riemann zeta function, including its Euler product on Re(s)>1, are admitted as analytic semantics. They are not claimed generated by the native fold primitives.

## Theorem
On Re(s)>1 the decoded transform D(s)=sum_(p,k>=1) log(p) p^(-ks) converges absolutely and locally uniformly and equals -zeta'(s)/zeta(s).
It has a unique meromorphic continuation to C. In the open strip 0<Re(s)<1, rho is a pole of D if and only if zeta(rho)=0. If that zero has multiplicity m, the pole of D is simple with residue -m.
Every admitted presentation satisfying H3 yields the same transform and continuation.

## Proof
For a compact set in Re(s)>1 choose delta>0 with Re(s)>=1+delta. Unique prime-power factorization identifies the absolute majorant with sum_n Lambda(n)n^(-1-delta), bounded by sum_(n>=2) log(n)n^(-1-delta), which converges by the integral test. The Weierstrass test proves local uniform convergence and holomorphy.
The absolutely locally uniformly convergent logarithmic Euler-product expansion is log zeta(s)=sum_(p,k>=1) p^(-ks)/k in this half-plane, choosing the branch defined by this expansion. Its derivative series is locally uniformly convergent by the same majorant. Differentiation gives zeta'/zeta=-D.
The quotient -zeta'/zeta is meromorphic on C and agrees with D on its initial half-plane. The meromorphic identity theorem gives uniqueness.
At a zero rho of order m, write zeta(s)=(s-rho)^m g(s), with g holomorphic and nonzero nearby. Then D(s)=-m/(s-rho)-g'(s)/g(s). Its residue is nonzero, so the pole is simple. Conversely, zeta is holomorphic throughout the open strip; where zeta is nonzero its logarithmic derivative is holomorphic. This proves the equivalence. Outside the strip the continuation also has poles at the trivial zeros and at s=1, where its residue is +1.
Exact decoding preserves each finite sum. Absolute convergence makes the limit independent of exhausting order, hence presentation-independent. Uniqueness preserves that equality after continuation. QED.

## What this establishes
Every nontrivial zero is represented by a pole in the collectively prime-derived transform. It does not choose one prime for that zero. Finite sums are entire exponential polynomials and have no poles; continuation, not substitution into a divergent series, supplies the critical-strip function. No assertion Re(rho)=1/2 is used or proved.

## ENIAD admission status
The additive prime detector has a source-level induction proof; saved finite input and transform comparisons provide supporting implementation evidence. H1-H3 for the complete encoded weighted family still require an explicit source-to-code correspondence. The existing address adapter preserves supplied analytic packets; it does not generate a zero address from a prime. This theorem is proved conditional on the stated interface, not a declaration that all interface premises are implemented.
